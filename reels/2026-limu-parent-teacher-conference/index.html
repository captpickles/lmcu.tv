Ledger note

What is established

Recovered through iSpot's March 14, 2026 Internet Archive snapshot, whose Open Graph metadata exposed the still-live public CDN master. The complete 30-second MP4 was downloaded and directly inspected without audible playback; its classroom action, speakers, title shot, paper text, and timing align with the independently preserved GDELT broadcast transcript. Doug and a woman presented as his wife attend a meeting with Doug Jr.'s teacher; the child appears behind them dressed as a miniature Doug, complete with mustache and uniform, and silently displays 'Insurense is dumb.' LiMu appears only in the opening title shot. Liberty Mutual's later private upload supplies an April 24 posting date, while iSpot supplies the earlier February 4 catalog date. The separate official `Mustache` upload remains an alias/possible alternate edit because its footage has not been recovered.
