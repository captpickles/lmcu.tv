Ledger note

What is established

Direct comparison proves Eric Molina's Garage portfolio master and iSpot's later-catalogued Quick and Easy master are the same assembly. Havas's contemporary report says Garage broke in November 2016; iSpot's November 1, 2017 date is therefore retained only as a later catalog/airing date, not the premiere. The reel cross-cuts a mother and two children at the waterfront with another mother chasing three boys through a repair garage; Charlie is named only as the temporarily missing brother.
