Ledger note

What is established

Directly watched from iSpot's public master. The mother's repeated savings pitch has displaced expected family words: the baby's first spoken word is Liberty, repeated for Mama, Auntie, and the final beat of the jingle. Official YouTube publication followed on January 2. An agency award entry confirms the mother, her sister, and the mother's firstborn relationship structure.
