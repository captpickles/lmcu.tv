Ledger note

What is established

Recovered from the authorized Vimeo embed in David Hoffman's portfolio and directly inspected without audible playback; its auto-caption track was checked against local transcription. Doug and his wife meet Doug Jr.'s teacher in an elementary classroom. LiMu appears in the opening title shot; Doug Jr. is discussed but not shown. Doug spends roughly twelve seconds forcing himself into a child-sized chair before asking how his son is doing. Vimeo's April 11 creator-upload date postdates Liberty Mutual's February 20 official upload and is not treated as the premiere. This directly verifies a premise distinct from the misspelled-paper/mustache Parent Teacher Conference reel. The April 24 Parent Teacher Conference (Short) description centers on the same undersized-chair gag and is probably a short cut of Tiny Desk, but edit identity remains unresolved until that footage is recovered.
