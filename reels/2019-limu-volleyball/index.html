Ledger note

What is established

Directly watched from iSpot's public master. Doug and LiMu play beach volleyball; Doug turns his serve into a sung motivational routine while LiMu produces a standard return, prompting the opposing team to call time out.

The July 26, 2024 date belongs to iSpot's catalog surface, not the reel's debut. Liberty Mutual marketing executive Jenna Lebel said in a 2021 interview that the spot ran in 2019 into 2020, and an October 7, 2020 viewer post explicitly describes the first 30 seconds as the television edit. The exact premiere date remains unresolved.

Butter Music + Sound also hosts a production-side Vimeo upload titled 'LiMu Emu & Doug Volleyball (30)' (video 505522429); its current privacy settings prevent direct metadata or edit comparison.
