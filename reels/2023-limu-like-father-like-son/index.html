What is established

Directly watched from iSpot's public master. Doug explicitly calls the boy his son and sends him out in a miniature yellow car with a juvenile emu. The boy calls LiMu “Uncle LiMu”; this establishes the form of address but does not by itself settle whether the relationship is literal or honorary. The juvenile emu's Lil LiMu label remains catalog/secondary metadata because it is not spoken in the cut.
