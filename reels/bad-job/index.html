What happens

A production failure enters canon.

Directly watched and transcribed from iSpot's public master. The original official YouTube upload is private; a separate contemporaneously hosted 1280x720 MP4 survives with a 2019-07-22 creation timestamp.

The actor flubs the company name, asks to riff, proposes emerging from the water, and finally produces “Liberty Biberty.” The director’s “We’ll dub it” confirms that the scene depicts the making of an ad inside the ad.
