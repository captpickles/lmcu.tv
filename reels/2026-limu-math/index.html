What is established

A surviving search-engine record preserves Liberty Mutual's verified official upload, exact title, February 20, 2026 publication date, and description: 'The curriculum has slightly changed since Doug was in school. But Liberty Mutual customizing car insurance so you only pay for what you need, has not.'

The upload is now private. Its current max-resolution thumbnail endpoint returns only YouTube's generic 120×90 unavailable placeholder, which was inspected but not archived as evidence. No runtime, caption text, moving master, or independent catalog entry has yet surfaced.

NoxInfluencer's July 9 cached analytics record preserves the official title/date and source tags including `doug jr`, `parent teacher conference`, `school`, and `teacher`. This establishes the Parent Teacher Conference production family more strongly than the description alone, but exact continuity or binary edit identity with Parent Teacher Conference, Tiny Desk, Mustache, or their shorts remains unresolved and is not asserted.

Two contemporaneous Reddit discussions preserve the joke's structure: Doug sees a classroom lesson and asks whether the children are being taught to bundle/save with Liberty Mutual; the teacher gently answers, 'No, math.' These are corroborating viewer recollections, not a recovered soundtrack, and their wording is not promoted to a verbatim transcript.
