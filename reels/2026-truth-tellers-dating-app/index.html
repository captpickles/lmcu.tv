Ledger note

What is established

Directly watched from Liberty Mutual's official upload and checked against its caption track. The Struggling Actor meets Marsha through an 'inter-web' dating app; she mirrors his idiolect, including 'phone-puter,' 'bondling,' 'Biberty,' 'la-zag-na,' and 'can-ooo-lis.' Their opening 'I am' exchange is audibly stylized but not respelled beyond the official captions. A stylized heart appears as they spin together. Little Black Book's embedded 30-second production master was independently recovered and silently frame-matched to this edit. Its production account calls this the first mini-campaign in a new season and says the wave was shot over two days in Los Angeles in mid-December 2025; Dating App went from shoot to shipment in eight days. Lindsay Foster's identification is corroborated by contemporaneous commercial coverage and her own reported social post, not by the reel credits.
