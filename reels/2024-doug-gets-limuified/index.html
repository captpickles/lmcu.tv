Ledger note

What is established

Directly watched from Liberty Mutual's official silent download and verified against its caption track. David Hoffman begins as Doug while makeup artist Jo Steel, herself costumed as Doug, applies a practical beak, face treatment, and feathers to turn him into a human LiMu imitation.

LiMu is the intended costume subject but the animal character does not appear as an independently depicted screen character. This human Halloween transformation is not evidence about the ordinary LiMu digital asset or final-pixel workflow.

Claims

What the evidence supports

  • BCopper Giants' award-entry account says the human LiMu Halloween transformation took roughly six hours and used zero special effects. The claim is scoped only to this practical makeup collaboration and must not be generalized to the ordinary animal LiMu character, whose established launch realization used a CG digital double.
  • BLiberty Mutual's official description directly links Jo Steel's time-lapse and brush Shorts as companion material to the full Doug Gets LiMu-ified film.
