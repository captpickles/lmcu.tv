Ledger note

What is established

Copper Giants' award entry and its February 24, 2026 LBB production report both describe another shorter reel in the Dating App mini-campaign involving a box of treats and a message whose letters taste like chocolate. February 24 is retained as a latest-attested existence bound, not assumed to be its public release date. The formerly mysterious private Truth Tellers playlist slot d6Pz0Evx7OQ is now excluded: its archived official watch page identifies it as an earlier upload of Dating App (Short). The award entry's five hidden full-resolution gallery images have now been recovered and inspected; all depict the watched 30-second Dating App reel and none shows treats or chocolate letters. A fresh inventory of the live official Truth Tellers playlist exposed nine public IDs, all already mapped to watched canon records, and no treats candidate. Agent Opus's April 30 Meta-ad snapshot contains several six- and ten-second assets promoted with `sweet tooth` and `make your day sweeter` copy. That language makes the paid-social group a plausible recovery lead for this scenario, but expired media and thumbnails prevent identification; no asset is assigned to this reel. Title, runtime, dialogue, release date, and original distribution surface remain unresolved.
